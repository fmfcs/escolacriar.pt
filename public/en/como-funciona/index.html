    • How CRIAR Works

    A school with clear routines, thoughtful organisation, and transparent communication.

    Here, everyday life is carefully structured to give children a sense of security and families confidence.

    Hours and Organisation

    The organisation of time at CRIAR combines structure and flexibility, respecting the children’s individual rhythms and the different stages of their educational journey.

    School Hours

    The school opens at 8:00 a.m. and closes at 6:00 p.m.

    Activity Hours

    • Activities for Preschool, Primary School, and Lower Secondary School start at 9:00 a.m.
    • Preschool finishes at 4:00 p.m.
    • Primary School and Lower Secondary School finish at 4:45 p.m.

    After these hours, and until 6:00 p.m., children can enjoy free play or take part in extracurricular activities in a calm and supportive environment.

    Food and Nutrition

    Food is an integral part of everyday life at CRIAR and is approached as part of the educational process.

    All meals are provided by the school, including:

    • morning snack,
    • lunch,
    • afternoon snack.

    Meals are prepared daily in the school kitchen, using mostly organic ingredients, respecting seasonality, and taking into account the children’s needs.
